# Scope: what this release demonstrates

## Data

All data are simulated: analysis-ready TDI A and E channels in the frequency
domain, one month long (T = 1/12 sidereal year), stationary Gaussian instrument
noise at the SciRD level, first-generation TDI with an analytic equal-arm
constellation, MBHB waveforms from PhenomD ((2,2) mode) with the BBHx fast
response, galactic binaries from GBGPU. The foreground has two parts: resolved
binaries injected as waveforms, and an unresolved confusion component drawn as
a stationary Gaussian realization of the confusion PSD. Gaps, glitches,
non-stationary noise and telemetry calibration are outside the simulation.

## Stage hand-offs

Stage 1 to stages 2 and 3. Two modes are available.

- `--detector oracle` (default; the configuration of the published end-to-end
  numbers). Every injected UCB receives a window whose centre is offset from
  the true frequency by a uniform error of at most 8 frequency bins, the centre
  error measured for the detector. The count passed to the alert is the number
  of injected UCBs. The detector is validated separately (`yolo/`).
- `--detector yolo`. The released detector scans the A channel of the same
  simulated data, and its detections define the windows and the count. Over
  three noise realizations of the Case-1 event it raised the MBHB flag every
  time, recovered 21/21, 23/26 and 19/19 of the injected UCBs above SNR 10,
  returned 0 to 2 detections without an injected counterpart, and its largest
  frequency errors were 2.9, 3.8 and 4.9 µHz; the residual after subtraction
  was 0.79, 0.63 and 0.35 of the noise level, against 0.51, 0.70 and 0.37 with
  the oracle windows and 4.8, 3.6 and 1.4 without subtraction.

Heterodyne reference. The alert flow is conditioned on a reference waveform
(chirp mass, mass ratio, spins, merger time) against which the data are
heterodyned. In an operational pipeline a search stage supplies it. In this
release it is drawn around the injected source with the training kernel at a
chosen scale (`--ref-scale`; 0.3% in chirp mass at scale 1), with the reference
spins drawn from the prior. The measured sensitivity of the alert to the
reference is the subject of the Supplementary Note "Stage-2 amortized inference
and the amortization floor" (`evaluation/probe_oracle_ref.py`,
`refine_infer.py`, `is_refine.py`).

Stage 3 to stage 4. The hand-off files contain the raw data, the recommended
PSD, the noiseless injection, the cross-code conventions and the alert
posterior. Stage 4 uses the alert posterior to initialize its chains and as a
Metropolis-Hastings proposal for the sky position, and never as a prior.

## Stage 4

Two sets of stage-4 results appear in the paper.

- Single realization (areas 191, 134 and 143 deg² at 90%; Fig. 2, Fig. 5a,b):
  alert-seeded parallel-tempered MCMC with 40 walkers and 10 temperatures,
  stretch moves and a Gaussian-mixture sky proposal, Gaussian likelihood with
  the PSD of the hand-off file. `pointing/synergy_pe.py` is this sampler. The
  chains are in `paper_products/pointing/`.
- Ensemble over four noise realizations with a hyperbolic likelihood and with a
  noise-fit Gaussian likelihood (Fig. 5c). These runs were made on a cluster
  with scripts of the HyperWave repository that are not part of this
  repository. Their hand-off inputs are generated by
  `pointing/run_ensemble.sh generate`, and their chains are distributed
  (`docs/DATA.md`), so the areas and the figure are recomputed here from the
  chains.

The sky coordinates of the stage-4 chains are in the frame of the HyperWave
template, which differs from the frame of stages 1 to 3 by a waveform-level
convention; `pointing/verify_conventions.py --map-truth` maps the injection
between the two by waveform matching.

## Coherent-grid refinement

`evaluation/recon_sky_test.py` is the feasibility study on emulated
reconstructions (Methods, "Reconstruct-then-localize"). It is a projection and
is not connected to the pipeline.

## Run modes of `heron/pipeline.py`

`--simulate` is the only data mode. A run on external data needs a search stage
for the heterodyne reference, which this release does not include.
